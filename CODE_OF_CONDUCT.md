# Code of Conduct

ReviewBus should be a technically rigorous and welcoming project. Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct, and protect information another person shares in confidence.

Do not use project spaces to shame maintainers, speculate about employment or availability, publish private information, or target individuals based on report metrics. Other unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, intimidation, sexualized attention, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, pause discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid public shaming.

Report conduct concerns privately through a verified contact method on the maintainer profile at <https://github.com/akigogikar>. Share only necessary information. Vulnerabilities and sensitive repository data use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project publicly.
